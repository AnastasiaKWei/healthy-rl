1Top bar — what is being measured: served model, vector version + probe layer, server health, job id/node, record count. "Copy tunnel cmd" gives the ssh -L line for the login node.
2Rail — every conversation this session, task runs (problem id, split, turns, pass/fail) and chats. Selecting one loads it into the centre and points the Trajectory/Tokens tabs at it. Session records persist under ARTIFACT_DIR.
3Conversation — the transcript. Assistant turns carry their start/end readout for the colour-by emotion; the tokens view tints every generated token by its cosine at the probe layer (blue negative, red positive; underline marks the readout token; thinking tokens sit in an amber band and are tinted the same way — affect in the trace is tracked, not hidden). Test-result messages are exactly what is fed back. The composer lets you intervene between attempts or just run the loop.
4Trajectory — headline tiles + per-turn line chart for the selected conversation, four replicating directions emphasised, the other ten toggleable in grey, session mean dashed behind. Cap warnings surface here because a capped turn is measuring the cap.
5Tokens — cosine per generated token for one turn, hover-linked to the transcript strip; layer and smoothing switches; a pinned-token detail card.
6Aggregate — session-wide table (mean at start/end, Δ last−first, SEM, p, n) filtered by source, plus mean-by-turn-index with SEM bands. Same conventions as live_trajectory.py: non-empty-turn indexing, non-finite skipping, stat/position switches.
